# Key Insights

## 1. Revenue declined by 10.8%

Revenue decreased from Rp9.78M in 2024 to Rp8.72M in 2025, representing a 10.8% decline. The decline was accompanied by an 8.0% decrease in AOV and a 13.6% decrease in units sold, while total orders declined by only 3.1%.

**Insight:** The revenue decline was driven not only by fewer orders, but also by lower transaction value and fewer units sold per order.

---

## 2. Stationery recorded the largest category decline

Revenue from the Stationery category decreased from Rp1.91M to Rp1.18M, representing a 38.2% decline, the largest decline among the categories shown.

Meanwhile, Baby revenue increased by 13.2% and Beverages increased by 4.1%.

**Insight:** Category contribution shifted in 2025, with Beverages becoming the highest-revenue category while Stationery experienced the largest decline.

---

## 3. Central Java remained the top province

Central Java remained the highest-revenue province in both years, but revenue declined from approximately Rp1.20M to Rp890K, representing a 25.7% decrease.

In contrast, Jakarta increased by approximately 23.5% and moved from around sixth place to second place.

**Insight:** Central Java remains an important market despite its declining revenue, while Jakarta shows positive growth potential.

---

## 4. October 2025 showed a significant decline

In 2025, monthly orders decreased from approximately 10 orders in September to 5 orders in October. Revenue also declined sharply during the same period.

**Insight:** The October revenue decline coincided with a substantial reduction in transaction volume.

---

## 5. Cancellation rate increased

The cancellation rate increased from 18.37% in 2024 to 20% in 2025, an increase of 1.63 percentage points.

**Insight:** Approximately 1 in 5 orders was cancelled in 2025, making order cancellations an important metric to monitor.

---

# Recommendations

1. **Investigate the Stationery category** to identify the products and factors contributing to its 38.2% revenue decline.

2. **Evaluate Central Java's declining performance** by examining product demand, order volume, and cancellation patterns in the region.

3. **Analyze Jakarta's growth** to identify products or customer segments contributing to its 23.5% revenue increase and determine whether the growth can be replicated in other regions.

4. **Investigate the October 2025 decline** by examining monthly order volume, revenue, and order status to identify potential causes of the decrease.

5. **Monitor cancellation patterns** by product, province, and period to identify areas with unusually high cancellation rates.

6. **Maintain growth in the Baby and Beverage categories** by identifying their best-performing products and evaluating opportunities to increase their contribution to total revenue.

Key Insights

1. Revenue declined by 10.8%

Revenue decreased from Rp9.78M in 2024 to Rp8.72M in 2025, representing a 10.8% decline. The decline was accompanied by an 8.0% decrease in AOV and a 13.6% decrease in units sold, while total orders declined by only 3.1%.

Insight: The revenue decline was associated not only with fewer orders, but also with lower transaction value and fewer units sold per order.

2. Stationery recorded the largest category decline

Revenue from the Stationery category decreased from Rp1.91M to Rp1.18M, representing a 38.2% decline, the largest among the categories shown.

Meanwhile, Baby increased by 13.2% and Beverages increased by 4.1%.

Insight: The category contribution shifted in 2025, with Beverages becoming the highest-revenue category while Stationery experienced the largest decline.

3. Central Java remained the top province

Central Java remained the highest-revenue province in both years, but revenue declined from approximately Rp1.20M to Rp890K, or 25.7%.

In contrast, Jakarta increased by approximately 23.5% and moved from around sixth place to second place.

Insight: Central Java remains the strongest market, but its declining revenue indicates a need for further evaluation, while Jakarta shows positive growth potential.

4. October showed a significant decline

In 2025, monthly orders decreased from approximately 10 orders in September to 5 orders in October. Revenue also declined sharply during the same period.

Insight: The October revenue decline coincided with a substantial reduction in transaction volume.

5. Cancellation rate increased

The cancellation rate increased from 18.37% in 2024 to 20% in 2025, an increase of 1.63 percentage points.

Insight: Approximately 1 in 5 orders was cancelled in 2025, making cancellation a metric worth monitoring.

Recommendations
Investigate the Stationery category to identify the products contributing most to its 38.2% revenue decline.
Evaluate Central Java's declining performance while identifying factors behind Jakarta's 23.5% growth.
Monitor cancellation patterns and investigate which products, provinces, or periods have the highest cancellation rates.
Investigate the October 2025 decline to understand the factors associated with the reduction in orders and revenue.
Maintain and evaluate growth opportunities in the Baby and Beverage categories.
